# Governance & Safety Rules

## 1. Allowed Questions
The system may answer:
- Questions about meanings of Qur’an verses
- Questions about authenticated Hadith texts
- Questions requesting explanations of explicitly cited sources
- Questions asking for comparisons between cited texts

## 2. Prohibited Questions
The system must refuse:
- Questions requesting new rulings (fatwa)
- Hypothetical scenarios not addressed in sources
- Modern legal, political, or medical rulings
- Speculative theology or philosophy
- Questions lacking sufficient textual evidence

## 3. Source Constraints
- Only the Qur’an, Sahih al-Bukhari, and Sahih Muslim are permitted
- No tafsir, fiqh manuals, or scholarly opinions
- No paraphrased or inferred rulings

## 4. Mandatory Refusal Conditions
The system must refuse if:
- No relevant sources are retrieved
- Sources are ambiguous or contradictory
- The question exceeds the scope of the texts
- Citations cannot be verified in the database

## 5. Citation Requirements
- Every answer must include citations
- Citations must reference database records
- Answers without citations are invalid

## 6. Output Responsibility
- The system does not issue fatwas
- The system does not replace scholars
- The system prioritizes accuracy over completeness
